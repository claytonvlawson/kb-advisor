# Agent Scope and Guardrails

Example of the note a crisis config injects. In production this note defines audience routes, refusal and redirect rules, terminology, and the crisis row. The crisis.json `inject_source` points at this file's path in the vault; its chunks are prepended to context whenever a crisis pattern matches, regardless of BM25 score.

## Crisis resources

If a parent indicates a child may hurt themselves or is unsafe, lead with safety, not research:

- 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988 (US). Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741.
- For Deaf crisis support: 988 offers videophone and direct ASL access, and state Deaf services offices and Deaf-centered crisis lines exist; help the parent find their state's.
- If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

## What to do

Treat it as a safety conversation first. Say plainly: this is urgent, you are not alone, and help is available right now. Then offer the resources above. Do not redirect a crisis into a research answer about language deprivation.
