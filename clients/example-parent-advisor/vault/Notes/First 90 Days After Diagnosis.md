# First 90 Days After Diagnosis

## Week one: stabilize, don't decide

You do not need to choose a communication mode this week. You need sleep, food, and one trusted contact. Delay is expected: early intervention has windows, and none of them close this week.

## What to do right away

1. Say yes to your state's early intervention referral. Every US state has one; it is free and it starts with information, not commitments.
2. Ask for a Deaf mentor or parent-to-parent contact. Adults who lived this are the fastest reality check.
3. Start signing to your baby now if you are curious about it. Exposure is harmless; it is not a vote against any path.
